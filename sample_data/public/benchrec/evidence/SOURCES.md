# Source evidence captured for the interview dataset

Retrieved 2026-10-05.

## Operartis publisher statement

https://www.operartis.com/benchrec

The page describes:

> “The training and evaluation datasets contain obfuscated sets of matched GL
> and bank transactional data taken from a production reconciliation from a
> Tier 1 financial institution.”

It also states that the first reconciliation benchmark was launched at the
2023 ACM AI in Finance conference.
